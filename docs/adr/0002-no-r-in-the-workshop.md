# No R in the Workshop

Every Tutorial is Python-only. This drops `hoodscanR` (the parent course's niche tool,
`003-downstream-analysis/3.1`) and the entire Seurat-based Visium/Xenium cell typing
module (`002`), both of which are good teaching material. An R kernel plus Bioconductor
installs in Colab would cost more of the two-hour slot than the content is worth, and
the parent course's `3.2_neighborhood.ipynb` already reimplements the niche concept in
Python.

## Consequences

Participants who work in R leave without a path from the Workshop into their own stack;
the Tutorials link `hoodscanR` and Seurat as further reading to partly cover this.
